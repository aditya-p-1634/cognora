# Cognora Vision

## One Sentence

Cognora is a continuity layer for human cognition.

## The Problem

Human thinking is fragmented.

Ideas appear, disappear, resurface, connect, evolve, and influence each other over time.

Most software stores thoughts as isolated documents.

As a result, continuity is lost.

## The Goal

Preserve continuity across time.

Help people understand:

- what matters
- what keeps returning
- what is unresolved
- what is connected
- how thinking evolves

## What Cognora Is Not

- Not a task manager
- Not a note-taking clone
- Not a knowledge graph
- Not a productivity dashboard

## What Cognora Is

A cognitive operating system.

Thoughts are living entities.

Relationships emerge naturally.

Influence becomes visible.

Continuity is preserved.

## Long-Term Vision

A user should be able to open Cognora after six months and immediately understand the current state of their thinking.
