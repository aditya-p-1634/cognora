# Influence Engine

## Purpose

Determine which thoughts occupy the center of the cognitive field.

Influence does not mean causation.

Influence means cognitive connectedness.

A highly influential thought is one that many other thoughts resonate with, connect to, extend, inform, or contrast.

---

## Influence Score Inputs

### Relationship Count

Number of connected thoughts.

### Relationship Strength

Aggregate relationship scores.

### Semantic Relation Weight

continues
extends
informs
contrasts

Weighted by cognitive significance.

### Recurring Concept Weight

Concepts that appear repeatedly across the workspace increase influence.

---

## Influence Bands

### Field Anchor

Thoughts that occupy the center of the cognitive field.

### Strong Influence

Thoughts that shape multiple regions of thought.

### Moderate Influence

Thoughts that influence a local cluster.

### Peripheral

Thoughts that remain loosely connected.

---

## Goals

Allow Cognora to identify:

* central thoughts
* influential concepts
* cognitive anchors
* relationship hubs

without exposing numerical metrics directly to the user.
